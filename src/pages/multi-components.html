Frequently Asked Questions

Standard passport processing takes 5-7 business days from the date of application submission. Express service is available for an additional fee, which reduces processing time to 2-3 business days. You can track your application status online using your application reference number. For a new passport application, you will need:
  • Original Emirates ID
  • Family book (original and copy)
  • Recent passport-sized photographs (white background)
  • Previous passport (if renewing)
  • Proof of payment for passport fees
All documents must be valid and clearly legible. Yes, you can renew an expired passport. The process is the same as renewing a valid passport. However, we recommend renewing your passport at least 6 months before expiry to avoid any travel disruptions, as some countries require passports to be valid for 6 months beyond your planned departure date. You can track your passport application in several ways:
  1. Online through our portal using your application reference number
  2. Via SMS by texting your reference number to 2020
  3. Through our mobile app available on iOS and Android
  4. By calling our customer service hotline at 600-522-222
You will receive SMS notifications at each stage of processing. Passport fees vary depending on the type of service:
  • New Passport (5 years): AED 250
  • New Passport (10 years): AED 500
  • Renewal: Same as new passport fees
  • Emergency Travel Document: AED 300
  • Express Service (additional): AED 150
  • Home Delivery (optional): AED 50
All fees are subject to change. Please check the latest fees before submitting your application.
