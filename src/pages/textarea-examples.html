API Reference

aegov-textarea

A flexible textarea component with character counter, resize modes, auto-resize, and clipboard copy support.

Pass-through Attributes

The following attributes are forwarded to the native <textarea> element: rows, cols, minlength, maxlength, wrap, autocomplete, autofocus, spellcheck, enterkeyhint, title, tabindex, form, plus all aria-* and data-* attributes.

6. Disabled & Readonly States

Disabled textareas are non-interactive and visually dimmed. The label inside aegov-form-field automatically dims when the textarea is disabled.

7. Readonly with Copy Button

When readonly and copyable are both set, a copy-to-clipboard button appears at the bottom-right corner. Text content is padded to avoid overlapping the button.
