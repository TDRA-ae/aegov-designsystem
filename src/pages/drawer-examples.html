Drawer Component

Off-canvas panel that slides in from screen edges. Supports 4 positions, 3 animation modes (slide, fade, none), pinned/unpinned appearance, and configurable overlay.

Basic Drawer (Right)

Drawer Title

This is a basic right-side drawer. It slides in from the right edge with a backdrop overlay.

Click the backdrop or the close button to dismiss.

 
<aegov-drawer id="drawer-basic">
  <div style="width: 320px; padding: 1.5rem;">
    <h3>Drawer Title</h3>
    <p>Content goes here</p>
    <button onclick="document.getElementById('drawer-basic').close()">Close</button>
  </div>
</aegov-drawer>

Animation Variants

slide

Smoothly slides in from the edge on open, and slides back out on close. Uses ease-in-out timing.

fade

Fades in on open, fades out on close. No slide movement.

none

Instant open and close, no animation.

Pinned vs Unpinned

Pinned

Flush to the edge with no margin or border radius.

Unpinned

Small gap from all edges with rounded corners. Creates a floating panel effect.

Overlay Control

Light Overlay (30%)

Subtle backdrop, content behind is more visible.

Base Overlay (50%)

Standard backdrop opacity (default).

Dark Overlay (70%)

Heavy backdrop for strong focus on drawer content.

No Overlay

No backdrop. Click the invisible area or press ESC to close.
